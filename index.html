I Can Help

Willing to help another traveller? Register the assistance you can offer and your preferred areas.

How it works: Motorists request help, and available helpers receive matching requests based on assistance type, location and geographic preferences. Our planned initial matching range is 75 km.
Important safety notice: Highway Help Canada is not an emergency service. If anyone is injured, in immediate danger, or involved in an emergency, call 911. Never put yourself or others in danger to provide assistance.
